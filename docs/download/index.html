One thing before you download

macOS will show a warning the first time you open Clipper. Here's why, and how to get past it.

⚠️

Clipper is not verified by Apple

Apple hasn't reviewed or "notarized" Clipper yet, so macOS will warn that it can't verify the app is free of malware and may call it harmful. That message appears for every app Apple hasn't checked, whether or not anything is actually wrong with it. Only install apps from sources you trust, and decide for yourself.

What you'll see

“Clipper” Not Opened

Apple could not verify “Clipper” is free of malware that may harm your Mac or compromise your privacy.

DoneMove to Trash

Click Done (not Move to Trash), then follow the steps below.

How to open it anyway

  1. Open the downloaded file and drag Clipper into Applications.
  2. Open Clipper from Applications. When the warning appears, click Done.
  3. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security and scroll down to Security. You'll see “Clipper was blocked to protect your Mac.” Click Open Anyway and enter your password.
  4. Open Clipper again and click Open. You only do this once. After that, allow Screen Recording and Microphone when asked.

Prefer Terminal? Run this once instead of steps 2–4: xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Clipper.app

Why isn't it verified?

Apple only verifies apps from developers who pay for a yearly Developer Program membership. Clipper is an independent app and doesn't have that yet. Updates installed from inside Clipper normally don't show this warning again.
